1. How does ChatGPT read?

ChatGPT doesn’t read sentences like we do.
It breaks text into small pieces called tokens.

💡Try a sentence, then see how it is tokenized!

Sometimes a word can be split into more than one token.

Tokens can be words, parts of words, or even punctuation.

The model works with tokens, not whole words.

Example:

playing→playing

cats→cats
